Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

scrobbledash displays your Last.fm listening statistics. We do not sell your data.

Data we use

When you connect your Last.fm account, we fetch your public scrobble data to generate stats and visualizations shown in the app.

Storage

Session and preference data may be stored locally in your browser. We do not store your Last.fm password.
